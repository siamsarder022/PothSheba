          Sensors find it, AI understands it, a live map shows it.

          "Poth" means road and "sheba" means service. PothSheba lets citizens report road and public-space problems on a live map, ranks them by priority and helps officers repair the most urgent first. A demo simulation of vehicle devices and a sensor pole can be switched on from the admin dashboard.

          System flow

          1. Sense: ESP32 device reads vibration (MPU6050) and GPS, and takes a photo on a sudden shake.
          2. Quick check: shakes under 1.5 g are dropped on the device.
          3. Send: event goes to the server (MQTT in the real system).
          4. Detect: photo confidence and vibration pattern are combined. Speed breakers are told apart from potholes.
          5. Confirm and merge: reports within 10 m and 24 h become one issue. 2+ reports make it Confirmed.
          6. Score: Score = 0.4 × Size + 0.3 × Traffic + 0.3 × Risk (near school, hospital, bus stop).
          7. Show and act: officer assigns repair, reporter sees the status.

          Severity levels

          Critical 75+, High 55+, Medium 35+, Low below 35. Weights are starting values to be tuned by testing.
